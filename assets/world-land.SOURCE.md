# World land polygons

- File: `world-land.geojson`
- Dataset: Natural Earth, 1:110m land, `ne_110m_land.geojson`.
- Primary repository: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Downloaded from: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Retrieved: 2026-10-31.
- License: public domain, verified against https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ .
- Use in this project: point-in-polygon classification of spherical surface particles and sampling coastlines in true 3D. The globe does not use a raster map or planet texture.
