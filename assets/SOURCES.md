# Asset sources

Retrieved on 2026-10-31 for the galaxy-to-Earth-to-Henan birthday scene.

## Particle Earth

The visible Earth is entirely three-dimensional particles. No Earth texture is used or shipped. Real land shapes are generated from Natural Earth data; see `world-land.SOURCE.md` for the upstream source and public-domain terms.

## Henan province boundary

- Local file: `henan.geojson`
- Provider: Alibaba Cloud DataV GeoAtlas public boundary data.
- Source: https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/410000.json
- Administrative code: 410000; province: Henan (河南省).
- Structure: GeoJSON FeatureCollection, one MultiPolygon feature, five polygons, 2070 coordinate pairs.
- Longitude/latitude order is retained exactly as supplied by the provider.
- Geographic extent: 110.360476 to 116.650320 E, 31.383448 to 36.365931 N.
- Supplied center: 113.665412 E, 34.757975 N.
- Supplied centroid: 113.619717 E, 33.902648 N.
- Geometry and properties are unmodified; no simplified or invented outline is substituted.
- The JSON endpoint does not carry a separate license declaration; no CC license is asserted here.

The particle cake in `../cake.js` is project-authored procedural geometry and uses no external image or audio assets.
